What it found

Two of your rules cancel each other out.

Neither document is wrong. They were written eighteen months apart by people solving different problems, and nobody has read them side by side since. Every agent session, and every new hire, resolves this on their own.

What happens next

The agent does not get to settle it.

A model asked to turn a messy standard into a clean rubric will happily reconcile the two and hand back something tidy. That is the failure. The contradiction is the finding, and it belongs to the people who wrote the rules.

01

It reads what you already wrote

CLAUDE.md, the contributing guide, the review checklist, the thread where someone settled a hard case.

02

It sorts, and refuses to repair

Enforceable rules become tests carrying the sentence they came from. Contradictions come back untouched.

03

Your team answers once

The answer goes back into the standard. Every session after that inherits it, instead of guessing again.
